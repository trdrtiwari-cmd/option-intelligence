Flexible ATR stop-loss and target

New signals use these multiples of the ATR measured at the moment of the signal; trades already logged keep the stop and target they were saved with. Each row records the ATR and the multiples used, so you can compare settings month by month. Owner account only.

Filter signal history

Only BUY signals are recorded: a CONFIRMED setup that held the reconfirm window and passed the option profit check. Each trade auto-exits at its stop-loss, its target, or the 15:20 square-off, whichever comes first, filled from the candles so a sleeping server cannot book a wrong price. Net P/L = gross minus the cost allowance.
