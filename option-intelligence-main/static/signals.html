Strategy results - net profit / loss

Every BUY signal (confirmed setup that also passed the profit check) is saved automatically with the option bought, its buy price, stop-loss and target, then auto-exited from the 1-minute price path. Net ₹ = premium move × quantity − charges; net points = index points − the cost allowance. Uses the date range and symbol filters in the next panel.

Flexible ATR stop-loss and target

New signals use these multiples of the ATR measured at the moment of the signal; trades already logged keep the stop and target they were saved with. The target is also brought in just before the next strong support / resistance (profit check). Owner account only.

Signal board - every option signal

Recorded automatically during market hours: every WATCH, setup (reconfirming), BUY, NO BUY (profit check failed) and FAILED signal, with the option chosen and its premium at that moment. About an hour later the board fills in what that option did over the next 60 minutes, and what a trade with the plan's stop / target (1.5 ATR / 1.5 ATR for signals that had no plan) would have done. The log is also copied automatically into your accounts database (set DATABASE_URL to PostgreSQL on Render) and restored after a restart, so the day and month history is kept. Exits are worked out from the 1-minute price path since entry, so a trade is closed at the level it actually touched even if the server was asleep at that moment (it is settled when the server wakes). A trade from an earlier session that cannot be settled from candles is marked EXPIRED rather than guessed.
