    Where it wins and loses

    Win-rate finder — exits with ≥ 70 % winners in BOTH halves

    An exit only counts when it wins ≥ 70 % of trades with a positive average in the first half AND the second half of the period.

    Opening Range Breakout (ORB) - a strategy the app does not use yet

    The high / low of the first 15 or 30 minutes after 09:15 is the opening range; the first 5-minute close above it is a CE entry, below it a PE entry; one trade a day, entries until 12:00. Three versions: the break alone, only when the engine leans the same way, and only when the range is 0.5-2.5 ATR wide. Live trailing exit (1.5 ATR stop), by half. Studies on US markets found it profitable; a replication on stock indices found the gain vanished after costs - so it is tested here on NIFTY before anything goes live.

    Strong-trend entries (the Signals page setting)

    The live rules buy pullbacks, so on a strong one-way day they never buy. A strong trend here means the last 20 candles moved at least 4 ATR in the trade's direction in a clean line (efficiency 0.6, or 0.55 for continuation) with price on the trade side of VWAP. "RSI up to 75" relaxes only the RSI limit. "Trend continuation" needs the candle to break the high (CE) / low (PE) of the last 3 candles and then relaxes the pullback rules (RSI up to 85, fresh breakout, up to 2 ATR beyond EMA 9, pullback trigger); never on a blow-off candle. Score, trend, higher timeframe, entry window and every other rule still apply. Same code as the live setting. Live trailing exit, by half.

    Leading triggers instead of the pullback trigger

    Three triggers that turn before the moving averages: RSI divergence, Stochastic cross out of oversold / overbought, MACD histogram turn. Strict: fires on a bar where every other rule already passed. Loose: fires with only the engine's side, the score and the entry window. "Extra" = trades the current rules did not take. Judged on the live trailing exit, by half. Set the winner live with LEADING_TRIGGER on Render only if it adds profit in both halves with 15+ trades.

    What-if: buy at support / sell at resistance reversals

    Entries where a bar touched a Medium / Strong support (CE) or resistance (PE) known before that bar, and closed back away from it as a reversal candle, inside the entry windows; one trade per level per day. Results with the live trailing exit, compared with the current signals. The "Distance from support" table in the groups below shows the same question for the signals actually taken.

    Entry time window by slot

    Setups blocked ONLY by the entry window (every other gate passed), split by when they happened. Judge each slot on the live trailing exit, in both halves of the period. A slot is worth opening only with 15+ setups positive in both halves.

    What-if: trend-day entries (test only - live signals unchanged)

    On a one-way day the price never pulls back, so the pullback trigger and the 12:00-14:30 gap block every entry. This replays a stricter "trend-day" rule: same side, score ≥ 70 and every other gate passing, only after 2+ earlier same-side setups of that day had already hit their 1.5 ATR target (no stop since), one trade at a time, max 3 a day. Results use the live 1.5 / 1.5 ATR exit. Only worth switching on if it is positive in BOTH halves with 15+ trades.

    Missed big moves — which gate held the signal back

    Clean moves the engine did not confirm in time, and the gates that were failing on that side when each move started.

    Gate check — setups blocked by exactly one gate

    If these would have paid more than confirmed signals, that gate is too strict. If they would have lost, it is doing its job.
